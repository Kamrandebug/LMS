Choose the word that is most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to COGENT.

💡 Explanation

Cogent means clear, logical, and convincing. Its opposite is 'unpersuasive' or not convincing.

Select the word that is the best antonym for DIATRIBE.

💡 Explanation

A diatribe is a forceful and bitter verbal attack. A eulogy is a speech that praises someone highly.

The word most opposite in meaning to PROFLIGATE is:?

💡 Explanation

Profligate means recklessly extravagant or wasteful. Frugal means sparing or economical with regard to money or food.

Which the best antonym for RECONDITE?

💡 Explanation

Recondite means little known or abstruse. Manifest means clear or obvious to the eye or mind.

Choose the word that is most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to SANGUINE.

💡 Explanation

Sanguine means optimistic or positive, especially in a difficult situation. Its direct opposite is 'pessimistic'.

Select the antonym for VENERATE.

💡 Explanation

To venerate is to regard with great respect or revere. To despise is to feel contempt or a deep repugnance for.

The word most opposite in meaning to PANEGYRIC is:?

💡 Explanation

A panegyric is a public speech or text in praise of someone or something. A condemnation is an expression of very strong disapproval.

Which word is the antonym of INSIPID?

💡 Explanation

Insipid means lacking vigor or interest; dull. Scintillating means sparkling, brilliant, and excitingly clever.

Choose the word that is most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to INCHOATE.

💡 Explanation

Inchoate means just begun and not fully formed or developed. 'Fully formed' is its direct antonym.

Select the word that is most opposite in meaning to ABERRANT.

💡 Explanation

Aberrant means departing from an accepted standard or what is normal. Its opposite is 'normal' or typical.

Choose the word that is most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to CACOPHONY.

💡 Explanation

Cacophony refers to a harsh, discordant mixture of sounds. Its direct opposite is euphony, which means a pleasing or harmonious sound.

The antonym for the word MISANTHROPE is:?

💡 Explanation

A misanthrope is a person who dislikes or distrusts humankind. A philanthropist is a person who loves and seeks to promote the welfare of others, making it the correct antonym.

Select the word that is most opposite in meaning to PARSIMONIOUS.

💡 Explanation

Parsimonious means extremely unwilling to spend money or use resources. Lavish means sumptuously rich, elaborate, or luxurious, implying generous spending, which is its opposite.

Which the best antonym for VERACITY?

💡 Explanation

Veracity refers to conformity to facts or the quality of being truthful. Mendacity means untruthfulness or the tendency to lie, which is the direct opposite.

Choose the word that is most nearly OPPOSITE in meaning to PEDANTIC.

💡 Explanation

Pedantic describes someone who is overly concerned with minor details and rules, often in a showy or ostentatious way. Simple, in the sense of being plain and unpretentious, is the most fitting antonym.

The word most opposite in meaning to EQUIVOCATE is:?

💡 Explanation

To equivocate is to use ambiguous language to conceal the truth or avoid commitment. To address something directly implies speaking with clarity and commitment, which is the opposite.

Which word is the antonym of ICONOCLAST?

💡 Explanation

An iconoclast is a person who attacks cherished beliefs or established institutions. A conformist is a person who follows traditional standards and established practices, making it the opposite.

Select the word that is the best antonym for VACILLATE.

💡 Explanation

To vacillate means to waver between different opinions or actions; to be indecisive. To remain steadfast means to be firm, resolute, and unwavering, which is the direct opposite.

Choose the word that is most opposite in meaning to ENGENDER.

💡 Explanation

To engender is to cause or give rise to a feeling, situation, or condition. To eradicate means to destroy completely or put an end to, making it a clear antonym.

The antonym for the word APATHY is:?

💡 Explanation

Apathy is the lack of interest, enthusiasm, or concern. Fervor is an intense and passionate feeling, making it the most direct opposite.
