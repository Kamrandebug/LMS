Question 1/20 🔥 0 streak

Which nitrogenous base is replaced by uracil in the structure of RNA?

💡 Explanation

In DNA, thymine pairs with adenine, whereas in RNA, uracil replaces thymine.

Who is considered the 'Father of Genetics' for his work on pea plants?

💡 Explanation

Gregor Mendel's experiments with pea plants established the fundamental laws of inheritance.

A human somatic cell contains how many pairs of chromosomes?

💡 Explanation

Human somatic cells have 46 chromosomes in total, which consist of 23 pairs.

Which of the following describes the phenotype of an organism?

💡 Explanation

Phenotype refers to the physical or biochemical characteristics of an organism, as determined by both genetic makeup and environmental influences.

What is the specific segment of DNA that codes for a functional product, usually a protein?

💡 Explanation

A gene is a distinct sequence of nucleotides forming part of a chromosome, the order of which determines the order of monomers in a polypeptide.

During DNA replication, which enzyme is responsible for 'unzipping' the double helix?

💡 Explanation

Helicase is the enzyme that breaks the hydrogen bonds between the base pairs of DNA, separating the two strands.

If an organism has two identical alleles for a particular trait, it is said to be:

💡 Explanation

Homozygous means having two identical alleles of a particular gene or genes.

Which nitrogenous bases are classified as purines?

💡 Explanation

Purines are double-ring structures (Adenine and Guanine), while pyrimidines are single-ring structures (Cytosine, Thymine, and Uracil).

What is the term for an alternative form of a gene located at a specific position on a chromosome?

💡 Explanation

An allele is one of two or more versions of a DNA sequence at a given genomic location.

In the famous Watson-Crick model, DNA strands are held together by:

💡 Explanation

Hydrogen bonds form between the complementary nitrogenous bases to hold the two strands of the DNA double helix together.

Which nitrogenous base pairs with Guanine in a standard DNA double helix?

💡 Explanation

In DNA, Guanine always forms three hydrogen bonds with Cytosine according to Chargaff's rules.

What is the structural unit of a chromosome consisting of DNA coiled around histone proteins?

💡 Explanation

A nucleosome is the basic repeating unit of eukaryotic chromatin, comprising DNA wrapped around a core of histone proteins.

Who proposed the 'One Gene-One Enzyme' hypothesis?

💡 Explanation

George Beadle and Edward Tatum proposed the one gene-one enzyme hypothesis, suggesting each gene encodes a single enzyme.

Which of the following describes the condition where both alleles of a gene are expressed equally in the phenotype?

💡 Explanation

In codominance, neither allele is recessive, and both are fully expressed in the phenotype, such as in AB blood type.

What is the term for the total genetic makeup of an organism?

💡 Explanation

The genome represents the complete set of genetic material present in an organism or cell.

Which enzyme is primarily responsible for synthesizing a new DNA strand during replication?

💡 Explanation

DNA Polymerase is the enzyme that catalyzes the addition of nucleotides to the growing DNA chain during replication.

A cross between a homozygous dominant parent and a homozygous recessive parent will produce an F1 generation that is:

💡 Explanation

A cross between AA and aa always results in 100% Aa (heterozygous) offspring in the F1 generation.

What are the constricted regions of chromosomes where spindle fibers attach during cell division?

💡 Explanation

The centromere is the specialized DNA sequence that links a pair of sister chromatids and acts as the site for spindle fiber attachment.

Which of the following is an example of a polygenic trait in humans?

💡 Explanation

Polygenic traits are controlled by two or more genes; human skin color is a classic example of continuous variation determined by multiple genes.

What is the process of producing an RNA copy from a DNA sequence called?

💡 Explanation

Transcription is the process by which information in a strand of DNA is copied into a new molecule of messenger RNA (mRNA).
