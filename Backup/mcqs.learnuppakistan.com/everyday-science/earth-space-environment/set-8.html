Which gas is primarily responsible for the depletion of the ozone layer in the stratosphere?

💡 Explanation

Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) release chlorine atoms when exposed to UV radiation, which break down ozone molecules.

The Kyoto Protocol is an international treaty aimed at reducing which environmental problem?

💡 Explanation

The Kyoto Protocol was an international agreement linked to the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change to combat global warming by reducing GHG emissions.

Which of the following is considered a primary source of 'acid rain'?

💡 Explanation

Acid rain is caused by chemical reactions of sulphur dioxide and nitrogen oxides with water vapor in the atmosphere.

What is the phenomenon where heat is trapped in the Earth's atmosphere by gases like CO2 and methane known as?

💡 Explanation

The greenhouse effect is the process by which radiation from a planet's atmosphere warms the planet's surface to a temperature above what it would be without its atmosphere.

The 'Red Data Book' published by IUCN contains information about:

💡 Explanation

The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species is the world's most comprehensive inventory of the global conservation status of biological species.

Which gas is the most abundant greenhouse gas in the Earth's atmosphere?

💡 Explanation

Water vapor is the most abundant greenhouse gas, though human activities primarily increase concentrations of CO2 and methane.

What is the main objective of the Montreal Protocol?

💡 Explanation

The Montreal Protocol was designed to phase out the production and consumption of substances responsible for ozone depletion.

Eutrophication is a process primarily associated with which type of pollution?

💡 Explanation

Eutrophication is the excessive richness of nutrients in a lake or other body of water, frequently due to runoff from the land, which causes a dense growth of plant life and death of animal life from lack of oxygen.

Which biome is known as the 'lungs of the planet'?

💡 Explanation

The Amazon rainforest is often referred to as the 'lungs of the planet' due to its massive role in absorbing carbon dioxide and producing oxygen.

Biomagnification refers to the increase in concentration of which substances as they move up the food chain?

💡 Explanation

Biomagnification is the accumulation of chemicals such as heavy metals or pesticides in an organism as a result of ingesting other plants or animals in which the chemicals are more widely disbursed.

Which environmental phenomenon is characterized by the accumulation of salts in soil, often resulting from excessive irrigation in arid regions?

💡 Explanation

Soil salinization occurs when water-soluble salts accumulate in the soil profile to a level that impacts plant growth, common in poorly drained irrigated lands.

What does the term 'Carbon Sequestration' primarily refer to?

💡 Explanation

Carbon sequestration is the long-term removal, capture, or sequestration of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere to slow or reverse atmospheric CO2 pollution.

Which chemical compound is the primary component of 'Photochemical Smog'?

💡 Explanation

Photochemical smog is formed when sunlight reacts with nitrogen oxides and volatile organic compounds in the atmosphere, producing ground-level ozone.

The 'Ramsar Convention' is an intergovernmental treaty focused on the conservation and sustainable use of:

💡 Explanation

The Ramsar Convention on Wetlands is an international treaty for the conservation and sustainable use of wetlands, signed in Ramsar, Iran, in 1971.

Which of the following pollutants is known to cause the 'Minamata disease'?

💡 Explanation

Minamata disease is a neurological syndrome caused by severe mercury poisoning, historically linked to the release of methylmercury in industrial wastewater.

What is the primary cause of 'Ocean Acidification'?

💡 Explanation

Ocean acidification is the ongoing decrease in the pH of the Earth's oceans, caused by the uptake of carbon dioxide from the atmosphere.

The 'Permaculture' concept, often associated with environmental sustainability, focuses on:

💡 Explanation

Permaculture is a design system for creating sustainable human environments by modeling them on the patterns and resilient features observed in natural ecosystems.

Which index is commonly used to measure the water quality of a river based on dissolved oxygen and biological indicators?

💡 Explanation

BOD measures the amount of dissolved oxygen required by aerobic biological organisms to break down organic material present in a given water sample at a certain temperature.

What is meant by the 'Anthropocene' epoch?

💡 Explanation

The Anthropocene is an informal term used by scientists to describe the current geological time period, in which human activity is the dominant influence on climate and the environment.

Which gas, produced by the incomplete combustion of fossil fuels, is highly toxic because it binds to hemoglobin in the blood, preventing oxygen transport?

💡 Explanation

Carbon monoxide (CO) binds to hemoglobin more strongly than oxygen does, forming carboxyhemoglobin, which reduces the blood's capacity to carry oxygen.
