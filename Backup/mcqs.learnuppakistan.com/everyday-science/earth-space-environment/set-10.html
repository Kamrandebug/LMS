Question 1/20 🔥 0 streak

Which scientific instrument is primarily used to measure atmospheric pressure?

💡 Explanation

A barometer is a scientific instrument used in meteorology to measure atmospheric pressure.

Who is credited with the invention of the first practical thermometer, known as the thermoscope?

💡 Explanation

Galileo Galilei is widely credited with inventing the thermoscope in the late 16th century, which was the precursor to the modern thermometer.

Which component of a compound light microscope is responsible for magnifying the image of the specimen?

💡 Explanation

The objective lens is the primary optical element that magnifies the specimen in a light microscope.

Which type of telescope uses a large concave mirror as its primary objective to collect and focus light?

💡 Explanation

A reflecting telescope uses a mirror to focus light, whereas a refracting telescope uses lenses.

What is the primary function of a mercury barometer?

💡 Explanation

Evangelista Torricelli invented the mercury barometer in 1643, which measures atmospheric pressure based on the height of a liquid column.

The resolution of a microscope refers to its ability to:

💡 Explanation

Resolution is the ability of an imaging system to distinguish two closely spaced points as separate entities.

Which scientific instrument was significantly improved by Hans Lippershey, leading to its widespread use in astronomy?

💡 Explanation

Hans Lippershey is often recognized for filing the first patent for the telescope in 1608.

What liquid is commonly used in traditional clinical thermometers due to its consistent thermal expansion?

💡 Explanation

Mercury was traditionally used in clinical thermometers because it expands and contracts linearly with temperature changes.

What is the main advantage of an electron microscope over an optical microscope?

💡 Explanation

Electron microscopes use beams of electrons instead of light, allowing for vastly superior resolution and magnification capabilities.

In a telescope, what does the 'aperture' refer to?

💡 Explanation

The aperture of a telescope is the diameter of the primary light-gathering element (mirror or lens), which determines its light-collecting power.

Which temperature scale is defined by the freezing point of water at 273.15 and the boiling point at 373.15?

💡 Explanation

The Kelvin scale is an absolute temperature scale where 0 K is absolute zero, making the freezing and boiling points of water 273.15 K and 373.15 K respectively.

An aneroid barometer measures atmospheric pressure by using:

💡 Explanation

An aneroid barometer uses a small, flexible metal box called an aneroid cell that expands or contracts based on external atmospheric pressure.

Which optical instrument allows for the viewing of tiny specimens by using two sets of lenses known as the objective and the eyepiece?

💡 Explanation

A compound microscope utilizes two lens systems (objective and ocular/eyepiece) to achieve high magnification of small specimens.

Refracting telescopes primarily rely on which optical principle to gather and focus light from distant objects?

💡 Explanation

Refracting telescopes use glass lenses to bend (refract) light rays to a focal point.

Who is historically credited with the first recorded use of a telescope for systematic astronomical observations?

💡 Explanation

Galileo Galilei is widely credited with being the first person to turn a telescope towards the night sky for scientific discovery.

In a clinical thermometer, what is the purpose of the 'constriction' in the glass tube?

💡 Explanation

The constriction (or kink) prevents the mercury column from receding into the bulb, allowing the user time to read the peak temperature.

Which part of a microscope controls the amount of light that reaches the specimen?

💡 Explanation

The diaphragm is located under the stage and is used to adjust the intensity and diameter of the light beam passing through the specimen.

A 'Barograph' is a specialized instrument that provides a continuous record of:

💡 Explanation

A barograph is a barometer that records the atmospheric pressure on a rotating drum or graph paper over a period of time.

What type of lens is used in a simple magnifying glass?

💡 Explanation

A simple magnifying glass consists of a single convex lens that creates a virtual, magnified image of an object.

Which instrument would be most suitable to detect the extremely faint light from distant galaxies?

💡 Explanation

Large aperture telescopes are designed to capture as much light as possible from faint, distant celestial sources.
