Which planet in our solar system has the highest surface temperature, despite not being the closest to the Sun?

💡 Explanation

Venus has a thick atmosphere rich in carbon dioxide, which creates a runaway greenhouse effect, making it the hottest planet in the solar system.

The asteroid belt is located between the orbits of which two planets?

💡 Explanation

The main asteroid belt is a region of space located roughly between the orbits of the planets Mars and Jupiter.

Which celestial body is officially classified as a 'dwarf planet' in our solar system?

💡 Explanation

Pluto was reclassified from a planet to a dwarf planet by the International Astronomical Union (IAU) in 2006.

What is the primary composition of the Sun?

💡 Explanation

The Sun is composed primarily of hydrogen (about 73%) and helium (about 25%), with small amounts of heavier elements.

Which planet is known as the 'Red Planet' due to the presence of iron oxide on its surface?

💡 Explanation

Mars appears reddish because its surface is covered in iron oxide, commonly known as rust.

Halley's Comet is visible from Earth approximately every how many years?

💡 Explanation

Halley's Comet has an orbital period of approximately 75 to 76 years, making it a short-period comet.

Which planet is famous for its prominent and complex ring system?

💡 Explanation

While other gas giants have rings, Saturn has the most extensive and visible ring system made primarily of ice and rock particles.

What is the term for the point in an object's orbit around the Sun where it is closest to the Sun?

💡 Explanation

Perihelion is the point in the orbit of a planet, asteroid, or comet at which it is closest to the Sun.

Which planet rotates on its side, with its axis of rotation tilted nearly 98 degrees?

💡 Explanation

Uranus has a unique axial tilt of 97.77 degrees, causing it to effectively rotate on its side as it orbits the Sun.

Which of the following is the largest moon in the solar system?

💡 Explanation

Ganymede, a moon of Jupiter, is the largest moon in our solar system, even larger than the planet Mercury.

The solar wind is primarily composed of which of the following?

💡 Explanation

The solar wind is a stream of charged particles (plasma) released from the upper atmosphere of the Sun, primarily consisting of electrons and protons.

Which planet in the solar system has the most extreme seasonal variations due to its orbital eccentricity?

💡 Explanation

Mars has a significantly elliptical orbit compared to Earth, causing dramatic temperature changes and dust storms depending on its distance from the Sun.

The region beyond Neptune that contains icy bodies and remnants from the formation of the solar system is known as:

💡 Explanation

The Kuiper Belt is a circumstellar disc in the outer solar system, extending from the orbit of Neptune to approximately 50 AU from the Sun.

Which layer of the Sun is visible to the naked eye during a total solar eclipse?

💡 Explanation

The corona is the outermost part of the Sun's atmosphere and is only visible during a total solar eclipse when the bright photosphere is blocked.

Phobos and Deimos are the natural satellites of which planet?

💡 Explanation

Phobos and Deimos are the two small, irregularly shaped moons orbiting the planet Mars.

What is the primary factor that prevents the asteroid belt from forming a single planet?

💡 Explanation

The massive gravitational pull of Jupiter prevents the material in the asteroid belt from coalescing into a single planet.

Titan, the second-largest moon in the solar system, orbits which planet?

💡 Explanation

Titan is the largest moon of Saturn and the only moon known to have a dense, substantial atmosphere.

The 'Great Red Spot' is a long-lived storm system located on which planet?

💡 Explanation

The Great Red Spot is a persistent high-pressure region in the atmosphere of Jupiter, producing an anticyclonic storm.

Which planet takes the longest time to complete one full revolution around the Sun?

💡 Explanation

Neptune is the eighth and farthest known planet from the Sun, making its orbital period the longest among the major planets at approximately 165 Earth years.

What are comets primarily made of?

💡 Explanation

Comets are cosmic snowballs of frozen gases, rock, and dust that orbit the Sun.
