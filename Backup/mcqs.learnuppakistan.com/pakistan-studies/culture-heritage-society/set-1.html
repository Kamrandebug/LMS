Which language is historically and linguistically considered the closest relative of the Sindhi language?

💡 Explanation

Linguistically, Sindhi and Saraiki are classified under the same northwestern group of Indo-Aryan languages, sharing significant common roots.

The 'Khowar' language is primarily spoken in which region of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Khowar is the primary language spoken in the Chitral district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and parts of Ghizer district.

Which script is used for writing the Balochi language in Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Balochi, like most major languages of Pakistan, is written using a modified version of the Perso-Arabic script.

Which dialect of Punjabi is predominantly spoken in the Pothohar plateau region?

💡 Explanation

Pothohari is a dialect of Punjabi spoken in the Pothohar plateau, which includes the Rawalpindi and Jhelum districts.

What is the primary linguistic classification of the Brahui language?

💡 Explanation

Brahui is unique in Pakistan as it is the only language in the region belonging to the Dravidian language family.

The 'Burushaski' language, spoken in Hunza and Nagar, is classified as:

💡 Explanation

Burushaski is considered a language isolate, meaning it has no demonstrated genealogical relationship to other known language families.

Which of these is NOT a recognised dialect of the Pashto language?

💡 Explanation

Siraiki is a distinct language, not a dialect of Pashto, which belongs to the Indo-Aryan branch.

Shina language is most commonly associated with which administrative territory of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Shina is the most widely spoken language in the Gilgit-Baltistan region of Pakistan.

The 'Majhi' dialect is considered the standard form of which major Pakistani language?

💡 Explanation

Majhi is spoken in the central districts of Punjab, including Lahore, and serves as the standard for written and literary Punjabi.

The 'Dameli' language, a member of the Dardic group, is primarily spoken in which district of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa?

💡 Explanation

Dameli is a Dardic language spoken in the Damel valley of the Chitral District.

Which dialect of the Saraiki language is considered the most widely used and standard form in the Multan region?

💡 Explanation

Multani is the primary and most standardized dialect of the Saraiki language centered in Multan.

The 'Torwali' language, belonging to the Kohistani branch of Dardic languages, is mainly spoken in which valley?

💡 Explanation

Torwali is an endangered Dardic language spoken by the Torwali people in the Swat and Bahrain valleys.

Which of the following languages is primarily spoken in the Lasbela district of Balochistan and is closely related to Sindhi?

💡 Explanation

Jadgali is an Indo-Aryan language spoken by the Jats of Lasbela, closely related to Sindhi and Kutchi.

The 'Gawri' language is a Dardic language spoken by the people residing in which northern areas of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Gawri (also known as Bashkarik) is spoken in the upper reaches of Swat and Dir districts.

Which of the following is considered a major dialect of the Hindko language spoken in the Abbottabad region?

💡 Explanation

Hazrawali is the distinct dialect of Hindko spoken specifically in the Hazara division, including Abbottabad.

The 'Wakhi' language, spoken in the upper regions of Gojal, belongs to which language family?

💡 Explanation

Wakhi is an Eastern Iranian language spoken by the Wakhi people in Gilgit-Baltistan.

Which dialect of Pashto is traditionally associated with the Kandahar region and is often considered the literary standard?

💡 Explanation

The 'Pakh'to' or Southern dialect is spoken in Kandahar and is generally considered the standard for literary Pashto.

What is the primary linguistic significance of the 'Balti' language in the context of Pakistan's languages?

💡 Explanation

Balti is a Tibetic language, closely related to Ladakhi, spoken primarily in the Skardu region.

The 'Marwari' language, spoken in parts of Sindh and Cholistan, belongs to which major language group?

💡 Explanation

Marwari is a Rajasthani language of the Indo-Aryan group, with speakers in the Thar desert region of Pakistan.

Which collection of Allama Iqbal was published posthumously in 1938?

💡 Explanation

Armaghan-e-Hijaz (The Gift of Hijaz) is the last poetic work of Iqbal, published after his death in 1938.
