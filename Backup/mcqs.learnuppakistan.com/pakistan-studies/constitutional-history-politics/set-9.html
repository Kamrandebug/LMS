The 'Doctrine of Necessity' was first invoked by the Supreme Court of Pakistan to validate the dissolution of the Constituent Assembly in which landmark case?

💡 Explanation

In 1955, the Federal Court validated the Governor General's dissolution of the Constituent Assembly using the doctrine of necessity, famously known as the Maulvi Tamizuddin Khan case.

Under the 1973 Constitution, who has the power to dissolve the National Assembly on the advice of the Prime Minister?

💡 Explanation

According to Article 58(1) of the 1973 Constitution, the President shall dissolve the National Assembly if so advised by the Prime Minister.

Which article of the 1973 Constitution pertains to 'High Treason' for abrogating or subverting the Constitution by use of force?

💡 Explanation

Article 6 of the 1973 Constitution defines the act of abrogating or subverting the Constitution by use of force or show of force as high treason.

General Zia-ul-Haq dismissed the government of Muhammad Khan Junejo in 1988 by invoking which article?

💡 Explanation

General Zia-ul-Haq used Article 58(2)(b), an amendment introduced by him, to dissolve the National Assembly and dismiss the Junejo government in May 1988.

The 'Agartala Conspiracy Case' was a significant political crisis during the era of which President?

💡 Explanation

The Agartala Conspiracy Case was initiated in 1968 by the Ayub Khan government against Sheikh Mujibur Rahman and others for alleged secessionist activities.

Which constitutional amendment abolished the President's discretionary power to dissolve the National Assembly under Article 58(2)(b)?

💡 Explanation

The 13th Amendment to the Constitution of Pakistan, passed in 1997, removed the power of the President to dissolve the National Assembly under Article 58(2)(b).

What is the primary function of a 'Caretaker Government' in the Pakistani constitutional framework?

💡 Explanation

The primary objective of the caretaker government is to maintain the status quo and facilitate the conduct of general elections within the stipulated constitutional timeframe.

The 'LFO' (Legal Framework Order) of 2002 was issued by which leader?

💡 Explanation

General Pervez Musharraf issued the Legal Framework Order in 2002 to modify the 1973 Constitution, providing himself with expanded powers including the revival of Article 58(2)(b).

Which supreme court verdict declared that the 'Doctrine of Necessity' could no longer be used to validate extra-constitutional actions?

💡 Explanation

In the 2009 Sindh High Court Bar Association case, the Supreme Court declared the emergency measures taken by Musharraf in 2007 unconstitutional and limited the scope of the doctrine of necessity.

Which political crisis led to the imposition of the first Martial Law in Pakistan on October 7, 1958?

💡 Explanation

The 1958 Martial Law was imposed by President Iskander Mirza citing the failure of parliamentary democracy and political instability.

The 'PCO' (Provisional Constitutional Order) was first introduced in which year to legalize the military takeover?

💡 Explanation

The PCO of 1981 was issued by General Zia-ul-Haq to restrict the judiciary's power and consolidate military rule.

Which landmark judgment in 1972 declared the Martial Law imposed by General Yahya Khan in 1969 as illegal?

💡 Explanation

The Supreme Court in Asma Jilani case overruled the Dosso judgment and declared the military regime of Yahya Khan illegal.

The 'Eighth Amendment' to the 1973 Constitution was essentially designed to:

💡 Explanation

The Eighth Amendment incorporated the RCO (Revival of Constitution of 1973 Order) and granted the President powers under 58(2)(b).

The dismissal of Benazir Bhutto’s first government in 1990 was executed under the provision of:

💡 Explanation

President Ghulam Ishaq Khan dissolved the National Assembly in 1990 using his discretionary powers under Article 58(2)(b).

In the context of constitutional breakdown, what is 'Indemnity Legislation'?

💡 Explanation

Indemnity acts are used by incoming regimes to provide legal immunity for the actions performed by a preceding military administration.

Which Prime Minister’s government was restored by the Supreme Court of Pakistan in 1993 after being dismissed by the President?

💡 Explanation

In 1993, the Supreme Court declared the dissolution of the National Assembly by President Ghulam Ishaq Khan as unconstitutional and restored Nawaz Sharif.

The 'LFO' (Legal Framework Order) 1969 was issued by which military ruler?

💡 Explanation

Yahya Khan issued the LFO 1970 (often referred to in the context of the 1969-1971 period) to define the framework for the 1970 elections.

Which article of the 1973 Constitution specifically deals with the proclamation of emergency due to external aggression or internal disturbance?

💡 Explanation

Article 232 provides the President the power to issue a Proclamation of Emergency if the security of Pakistan is threatened.

The 'Constitutional Crisis' of 1954 involved the dissolution of the Constituent Assembly by which Governor General?

💡 Explanation

In October 1954, Governor General Ghulam Muhammad dissolved the Constituent Assembly, which triggered a major constitutional crisis.

Which Prime Minister of Pakistan was the first to be dismissed by the Governor General in 1953, setting a precedent for civil-military instability?

💡 Explanation

Governor General Ghulam Muhammad dismissed Khawaja Nazimuddin in 1953 despite his majority in the Constituent Assembly, marking the beginning of bureaucratic-military intervention in civilian politics.
