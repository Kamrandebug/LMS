Question 1/19 🔥 0 streak

The Indus Plain is primarily composed of which type of soil?

💡 Explanation

The Indus Plain is a vast fertile plain formed by the deposition of silt and sediments (alluvium) by the Indus River and its tributaries.

Which of the following plateaus is located in the province of Balochistan, Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Kachhi Plateau (or plain) is a lowland basin located in Balochistan, Pakistan.

What is the primary characteristic of a 'Piedmont Plain'?

💡 Explanation

A Piedmont plain is a plain formed at the foot of a mountain range by the accumulation of debris transported by streams.

Which desert in Pakistan is located between the Indus and Jhelum rivers?

💡 Explanation

The Thal Desert is situated in the Punjab province of Pakistan, specifically located between the Indus and Jhelum rivers.

Deosai Plateau is located in which region of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Deosai National Park is a high-altitude alpine plateau located in the Skardu District of Gilgit-Baltistan.

Which of these is the largest desert in the world?

💡 Explanation

The Antarctic Desert is the largest desert in the world, classified as a polar desert.

The Potwar Plateau is bounded by which two rivers?

💡 Explanation

The Potwar Plateau is situated in northern Punjab, Pakistan, bordered by the Indus River to the west and the Jhelum River to the east.

The Thal Desert is situated between which two rivers of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Thal Desert is located in the Punjab province of Pakistan, situated between the Indus and Jhelum rivers.

Which of the following is a 'cold desert' located in the Gilgit-Baltistan region of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Katpana Desert is a high-altitude cold desert located near Skardu in Gilgit-Baltistan.

The Pothohar Plateau is famous for the presence of which major mineral resource?

💡 Explanation

The Pothohar Plateau, located in northern Punjab, is the primary region for oil and gas exploration in Pakistan.

Which desert is located in the Balochistan province of Pakistan and is known for its salt pans?

💡 Explanation

The Kharan Desert is situated in the Balochistan province and is characterized by sand dunes and salt basins.

The Indo-Gangetic Plain is classified as which type of plain?

💡 Explanation

The Indo-Gangetic Plain is a vast alluvial plain formed by the sediments deposited by the Indus and Ganges river systems.

What is the common term for the elevated, flat-topped landform that rises abruptly from surrounding lowlands?

💡 Explanation

A plateau, also called a tableland, is an area of highland, usually consisting of relatively flat terrain.

The 'Rohri Hills' are a prominent feature located within which region?

💡 Explanation

The Rohri Hills are a limestone ridge system located in the Lower Indus Plain in the Sindh province.

Which physical region of Pakistan is characterized by deep ravines and dissected landscapes?

💡 Explanation

The Potwar Plateau is known for its rugged terrain, dissected by extensive erosion and deep ravines formed by seasonal streams.

The Karakoram range borders which major plateau to the north?

💡 Explanation

The Tibetan Plateau is situated to the north of the Himalayan and Karakoram mountain ranges.

What is the name of the desert that extends across both India and Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Thar Desert, also known as the Great Indian Desert, spans across the border regions of India and Pakistan.

Which mountain range separates the provinces of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Gilgit-Baltistan from the rest of the country?

💡 Explanation

The Hindu Kush range is predominantly located in the northern regions of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and connects with the Pamir Knot, acting as a major physical barrier.

Which desert, known as the 'Cholistan', is located in the southern part of the Punjab province?

💡 Explanation

The Cholistan Desert, locally known as Rohi, covers an area of approximately 26,000 square kilometers in Punjab.
