Which animal is the national animal of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Markhor (Capra falconeri) is the national animal of Pakistan and is found in the northern mountainous regions.

In which province is the Juniper forest of Ziarat located?

💡 Explanation

The Juniper forests in Ziarat, Balochistan, are among the oldest and largest in the world.

Which organization is primarily responsible for the management of wildlife and biodiversity in Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Ministry of Climate Change oversees environmental policies, including the conservation of wildlife and biodiversity in Pakistan.

Which of the following birds is known as the national bird of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Chakor (Alectoris chukar) is the national bird of Pakistan, representing resilience and beauty.

The 'Himalayan National Park' is located in which region of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Central Karakoram National Park and Deosai National Park are significant wildlife habitats located in Gilgit-Baltistan.

What is the primary cause of deforestation in the northern areas of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Illegal logging by the timber mafia and local dependence on wood for heating and cooking are the primary drivers of deforestation in northern Pakistan.

Which endangered cat species is primarily found in the high altitude areas of Northern Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Snow Leopard is an endangered species that inhabits the rugged mountain ranges of the Himalayas, Karakoram, and Hindu Kush in Pakistan.

Which act serves as the legal framework for the protection of wildlife in Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Pakistan Wildlife (Protection, Preservation, Conservation and Management) Act, 1974 provides the foundational legal framework for wildlife conservation.

The 'Changa Manga' forest, one of the largest man-made forests in the world, is located in which district of Punjab?

💡 Explanation

Changa Manga is a man-made forest located in the Kasur District of Punjab, Pakistan.

Which of the following is considered the primary 'Carbon Sink' in Pakistan's biodiversity hotspots?

💡 Explanation

The coniferous forests of the northern areas of Pakistan store significant amounts of carbon, acting as vital carbon sinks.

The 'Margalla Hills National Park' was established in which year?

💡 Explanation

Margalla Hills National Park was established in 1980 and covers an area of approximately 17,386 hectares.

Which plant species is primarily used in the 'Ten Billion Tree Tsunami' project to combat desertification?

💡 Explanation

Acacia nilotica (Kikar) is native to Pakistan and is extensively used in reforestation projects due to its resilience.

The 'Blind Indus Dolphin' is a protected species primarily found in which river ecosystem?

💡 Explanation

The Indus River Dolphin (Platanista minor) is an endangered freshwater dolphin found in the Indus River system.

What is the primary ecological significance of the Mangrove forests in the Indus Delta?

💡 Explanation

Mangroves serve as a critical buffer zone against coastal storms, tsunamis, and soil erosion in the Indus Delta.

The 'Houbara Bustard', a migratory bird, is famous for its winter migration to which region of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

The Houbara Bustard migrates from Central Asia to the warmer desert regions of Pakistan, particularly in Balochistan and Sindh.

Which of the following mountain ranges in Pakistan is home to the highest concentration of biodiversity in terms of Alpine flora?

💡 Explanation

The Himalayan range in Pakistan features a wide variety of alpine meadows and flora due to its diverse altitude zones.

The 'Chiltan Ibex' is a subspecies of wild goat found exclusively in which national park?

💡 Explanation

The Chiltan Ibex is a rare species endemic to the Hazarganji-Chiltan National Park in Balochistan.

What is the main objective of the 'Convention on Biological Diversity' (CBD) to which Pakistan is a signatory?

💡 Explanation

The CBD aims to conserve biological diversity and promote the sustainable use of its components.

Which of the following crops is classified as a Kharif crop in Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Rice is a major Kharif (summer) crop in Pakistan, sown in summer and harvested in autumn.

What is the primary factor that makes the Punjab province the 'breadbasket' of Pakistan?

💡 Explanation

Punjab's vast and historical network of canals (the Indus Basin Irrigation System) is the backbone of its agricultural productivity.
