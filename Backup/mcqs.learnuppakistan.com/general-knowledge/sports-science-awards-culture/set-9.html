Who was the first person to reach the South Pole?

💡 Explanation

Norwegian explorer Roald Amundsen led the first expedition to reach the South Pole on December 14, 1911.

Which world leader was awarded the Bharat Ratna, the highest civilian award of India?

💡 Explanation

Nelson Mandela was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1990 for his role in the anti-apartheid movement.

Who is credited with inventing the World Wide Web in 1989?

💡 Explanation

Tim Berners-Lee, a British scientist, invented the World Wide Web while working at CERN.

Which author is famous for the epic 'War and Peace'?

💡 Explanation

Leo Tolstoy, a Russian novelist, authored the literary masterpiece War and Peace.

Who was the first female Prime Minister of any country in the world?

💡 Explanation

Sirimavo Bandaranaike of Sri Lanka became the world's first female Prime Minister in 1960.

Florence Nightingale is famously known by which title?

💡 Explanation

Florence Nightingale gained the nickname 'The Lady with the Lamp' during the Crimean War.

Who is the longest-serving monarch in British history?

💡 Explanation

Queen Elizabeth II reigned for 70 years and 214 days, surpassing Queen Victoria.

Which scientist developed the theory of natural selection?

💡 Explanation

Charles Darwin proposed the theory of evolution by natural selection in his book 'On the Origin of Species'.

Who is the only person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields: Physics (1903) and Chemistry (1911)?

💡 Explanation

Marie Curie is the first person and the only woman to win the Nobel Prize twice, and the only person to win the Nobel Prize in two different scientific fields.

Who was the first person to walk on the moon during the Apollo 11 mission in 1969?

💡 Explanation

Neil Armstrong was the American astronaut who became the first human to step onto the lunar surface on July 20, 1969.

Which civil rights leader delivered the famous 'I Have a Dream' speech during the March on Washington in 1963?

💡 Explanation

Martin Luther King Jr. delivered his iconic 'I Have a Dream' speech from the steps of the Lincoln Memorial.

Who is the legendary footballer known as 'The King of Football' and the only player to win three FIFA World Cups?

💡 Explanation

Pelé, the Brazilian football icon, remains the only player in history to have won three FIFA World Cup titles.

Which personality founded the 'Missionaries of Charity' and was awarded the Nobel Peace Prize in 1979?

💡 Explanation

Mother Teresa founded the Missionaries of Charity and devoted her life to serving the poor, receiving the Nobel Peace Prize for her humanitarian work.

Who is recognized as the primary author of the theory of evolution by natural selection as detailed in 'On the Origin of Species'?

💡 Explanation

Charles Darwin published 'On the Origin of Species' in 1859, which introduced the scientific theory that populations evolve over generations through natural selection.

Which political leader spent 27 years in prison before becoming the first black President of South Africa?

💡 Explanation

Nelson Mandela was a South African anti-apartheid revolutionary who served 27 years in prison and later became the nation's first black head of state.

Who was the first woman to fly solo across the Atlantic Ocean?

💡 Explanation

Amelia Earhart became the first female aviator to fly solo across the Atlantic Ocean in 1932.

Which mathematician is considered the father of computer science and designed the 'Turing Machine'?

💡 Explanation

Alan Turing was a British mathematician who formulated the concept of the Turing machine, laying the foundations for modern computing.

Who is the Italian polymath known for painting the 'Mona Lisa' and 'The Last Supper'?

💡 Explanation

Leonardo da Vinci was a Renaissance genius, scientist, and artist famous for his masterpieces including the Mona Lisa.

Which archaeological site in Pakistan was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 1980, known for its well-planned grid system?

💡 Explanation

Mohenjo-Daro was the first site in Pakistan to be inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1980.

The 'Rohtas Fort', built by Sher Shah Suri, is located in which district of Punjab?

💡 Explanation

Rohtas Fort is a major military architecture landmark located near Dina in the Jhelum District.
