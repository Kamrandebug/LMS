The literal meaning of the term 'Tauheed' is:

💡 Explanation

Tauheed is derived from the Arabic word 'Wahhada', which means to make something one or to assert oneness.

Which category of Tauheed refers to the belief that Allah is the sole Creator, Sustainer, and Owner of the universe?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-ar-Rububiyyah is the Oneness of Lordship, meaning Allah is the sole Creator, Maintainer, and Master of all existence.

Singling out Allah alone for worship, such as prayer, fasting, and sacrifice, is known as:

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-al-Uluhiyyah (or Tauheed-al-Ibadah) signifies that all acts of worship must be directed exclusively to Allah.

Believing in Allah's names and attributes as described in the Quran and Sunnah without equating them to creation is called:

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-al-Asma-was-Sifat relates to affirming the names and attributes of Allah as they are, without human interpretation, alteration, or comparison.

Which of the following is considered the exact opposite of Tauheed?

💡 Explanation

Shirk (associating partners with Allah) is the antithesis of Tauheed and is considered the gravest sin in Islam.

The phrase 'La Ilaha Illallah' most fundamentally establishes:

💡 Explanation

The Kalimah establishes the rejection of all false deities and the assertion that worship belongs only to Allah, which is the essence of Tauheed-al-Uluhiyyah.

Which Surah of the Holy Quran is considered the 'Surah of Tauheed' because it exclusively discusses Allah's nature?

💡 Explanation

Surah Al-Ikhlas (Chapter 112) is known as the essence of Tauheed as it defines the Oneness of Allah.

Assigning attributes of Allah to created beings is a violation of:

💡 Explanation

To equate the attributes of the creation with the unique attributes of the Creator is a corruption of Tauheed-al-Asma-was-Sifat.

The belief that Allah is the only Judge and Legislator is part of which aspect of Tauheed?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-al-Hakimiyyah refers to the sovereignty of Allah in legislation and judgment, often considered a subset of Tauheed-al-Uluhiyyah.

Which of the following is not a category of Tauheed generally defined by Islamic scholars?

💡 Explanation

The standard categorization of Tauheed consists of Rububiyyah, Uluhiyyah, and Asma-was-Sifat; Tauheed-al-Malaikah is not a recognized category.

Which term describes the act of associating partners with Allah, considered the greatest sin in Islam?

💡 Explanation

Shirk is the theological opposite of Tauheed, involving the attribution of divinity or divine attributes to anyone or anything other than Allah.

Tauheed-al-Asma-wa-Sifat relates specifically to which aspect of Allah?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-al-Asma-wa-Sifat is the belief in Allah's names and attributes as they appear in the Quran and Sunnah without denial, distortion, or comparison to creation.

The declaration 'La Ilaha Illallah' signifies the negation of deity to anyone except Allah, a concept technically known as:

💡 Explanation

The Kalimah consists of Nafee (negation of all false gods) and Ithbat (affirmation of Allah as the only deity).

Believing that Allah has no equal or peer in His essence and kingdom falls under which primary category?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-ur-Rububiyyah refers to Allah's lordship, power, and uniqueness in His sovereign control and essence.

Committing an act of worship for anyone other than Allah, such as making a vow at a grave, violates which category of Tauheed?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-al-Uluhiyyah relates to the right of Allah to be worshiped alone. Any act of worship directed elsewhere is a breach of this category.

The concept that 'there is none comparable to Him' (Laisa kamithlihi shay) is a foundational principle for:

💡 Explanation

This Quranic verse from Surah Ash-Shura prohibits comparing Allah's attributes to created beings, a core tenet of Tauheed-al-Asma-wa-Sifat.

Which category of Tauheed is specifically violated by those who claim the right to legislate laws contrary to the Sharia?

💡 Explanation

Tauheed-ul-Hakimiyyah is the recognition that sovereignty, supreme law-making authority, and the right to legislate belong solely to Allah.

What is 'Shirk-ul-Asghar' (Minor Shirk) often associated with?

💡 Explanation

Minor shirk includes acts like Riya, where an act of worship is performed to impress others rather than purely for Allah.

Which of these is a pillar of the belief in Tauheed according to the classification of Islamic scholars?

💡 Explanation

The traditional tripartite classification of Tauheed is into Rububiyyah (Lordship), Uluhiyyah (Worship), and Asma-wa-Sifat (Names and Attributes).

The belief that Allah is 'Al-Hayy' (The Ever-Living) and 'Al-Qayyum' (The Sustainer) is an expression of:

💡 Explanation

Attributing names and attributes like The Ever-Living and The Sustainer to Allah is the primary focus of Tauheed-al-Asma-wa-Sifat.
