Question 1/20 🔥 0 streak

Who was the founder of the Umayyad Caliphate?

💡 Explanation

Muawiyah I established the Umayyad dynasty in 661 AD after the abdication of Hazrat Hasan ibn Ali (RA).

The Umayyad capital was shifted from Madinah to which city?

💡 Explanation

Muawiyah I moved the capital of the Islamic Caliphate from Madinah to Damascus, which remained the seat of power throughout the Umayyad period.

Who was the Umayyad general responsible for the conquest of Spain (Al-Andalus) in 711 AD?

💡 Explanation

Tariq ibn Ziyad led the initial Muslim forces across the Strait of Gibraltar into the Iberian Peninsula in 711 AD.

Which Umayyad Caliph is credited with the 'Arabization' of the administration and the introduction of the first purely Islamic coinage?

💡 Explanation

Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan instituted reforms that made Arabic the official language of government and established the gold dinar.

Who was the young general who conquered Sindh in 712 AD during the Umayyad period?

💡 Explanation

Muhammad ibn Qasim, under the orders of Hajjaj ibn Yusuf, conquered the Sindh region during the reign of Walid I.

The Umayyad Caliph Umar ibn Abd al-Aziz (Umar II) is famously remembered for:

💡 Explanation

Umar II is often called the fifth Rightly Guided Caliph due to his exceptional piety, justice, and social reforms.

Which famous building, often known as the Dome of the Rock, was constructed by the Umayyad Caliph Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan?

💡 Explanation

The Dome of the Rock (Qubbat al-Sakhrah) was completed in 691-692 AD in Jerusalem by Abd al-Malik ibn Marwan.

What was the official language of the state during the Umayyad Caliphate after the reforms of Abd al-Malik?

💡 Explanation

Abd al-Malik mandated the use of Arabic for all administrative records, replacing the previous local languages.

The Battle of Tours (732 AD), which stopped the Muslim expansion into Western Europe, took place during the reign of which Umayyad Caliph?

💡 Explanation

Hisham ibn Abd al-Malik was the Caliph when the forces of Charles Martel defeated the Umayyad army at the Battle of Tours.

The Umayyad dynasty was overthrown in 750 AD by which movement?

💡 Explanation

The Abbasids, who were descendants of the Prophet's uncle Abbas, launched a revolution that ended Umayyad rule in 750 AD.

Which Umayyad Caliph is considered the first to establish a professional naval fleet to challenge the Byzantine dominance in the Mediterranean?

💡 Explanation

Muawiyah I, as the governor of Syria and later Caliph, recognized the importance of naval power and initiated the construction of the first Islamic navy.

Qutayba ibn Muslim, a prominent Umayyad commander, is best known for his successful campaigns in which region?

💡 Explanation

Qutayba ibn Muslim extended the Umayyad frontiers deep into Central Asia, conquering cities like Bukhara and Samarkand.

During the Umayyad period, which city became the primary base for the expansion into North Africa and the subsequent launch point for the conquest of Spain?

💡 Explanation

Kairouan, founded by Uqba ibn Nafi, served as the central garrison and administrative hub for Islamic expansion across the Maghreb.

The Umayyad Caliphate faced a major internal revolt led by which figure, eventually resulting in the Battle of Karbala?

💡 Explanation

The Battle of Karbala occurred in 680 AD during the reign of Yazid I, marking a pivotal turning point in Islamic history.

Who was the legendary commander sent by the Umayyads to capture Constantinople in the major siege of 717-718 AD?

💡 Explanation

Maslama ibn Abd al-Malik led the Umayyad forces in the prolonged and unsuccessful siege of Constantinople during the reign of Caliph Sulayman.

Which Umayyad Caliph commissioned the construction of the Great Mosque of Damascus, also known as the Umayyad Mosque?

💡 Explanation

Walid I oversaw the transformation of the site of the Basilica of Saint John the Baptist into the architectural masterpiece known as the Great Mosque of Damascus.

The 'Second Fitna' (civil war) occurred primarily due to the contested succession after the death of which Umayyad Caliph?

💡 Explanation

The death of Yazid I in 683 AD triggered a major power struggle known as the Second Fitna, involving claimants like Hazrat Abdullah ibn Zubayr (RA).

Which group of people, initially serving as administrators, were granted significant positions during the Umayyad period before the rise of Persian influence?

💡 Explanation

The Umayyads retained many Byzantine administrative systems and personnel, especially in Syria, to manage the complex bureaucracy.

The Umayyad policy of 'Mawali' referred to which of the following groups?

💡 Explanation

Mawali were non-Arab converts to Islam who often held a lower social status than Arab Muslims during the early Umayyad period.

In the administrative divisions of the Umayyad Caliphate, what was the term used for the provincial governors?

💡 Explanation

Amirs were the provincial governors appointed by the Caliph to oversee the administration and military affairs of their respective regions.
