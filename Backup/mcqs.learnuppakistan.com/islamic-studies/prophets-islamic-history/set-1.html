Question 1/20 🔥 0 streak

In which cave did Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) receive the first revelation of the Quran?

💡 Explanation

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) received the first revelation (Surah Al-Alaq) in the Cave of Hira, located on the Mountain of Light (Jabal al-Nour).

Who was the first adult male to accept Islam during the Makkan period?

💡 Explanation

Hazrat Abu Bakr as-Siddiq (RA) was the first adult male to embrace Islam at the invitation of the Prophet (PBUH).

What was the name of the tribe to which the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) belonged?

💡 Explanation

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) belonged to the Banu Hashim clan of the Quraish tribe.

Which year of the Prophethood is known as 'Am-ul-Huzn' (The Year of Sorrow)?

💡 Explanation

The 10th year of Prophethood is called the Year of Sorrow because Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) lost his wife Hazrat Khadija (RA) and his uncle Abu Talib.

What is the name of the journey in which the Prophet (PBUH) traveled from Makkah to Jerusalem and then ascended to the heavens?

💡 Explanation

Isra wal-Mi'raj refers to the night journey from Makkah to Jerusalem and the subsequent ascension to the heavens.

Which companion of the Prophet (PBUH) was martyred after being forced to lie on burning sand during the early Makkan persecutions?

💡 Explanation

Bilal ibn Rabah (RA) was tortured by his master Umayyah ibn Khalaf, who forced him to lie on the hot burning sands of Makkah.

Who was the wet nurse of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) who belonged to the Banu Sa'd tribe?

💡 Explanation

Halima Sadia was the wet nurse of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) who raised him in the desert of Banu Sa'd.

What was the significance of the 'Boycott of Banu Hashim' during the Makkan period?

💡 Explanation

The Quraish imposed a severe socio-economic boycott on the Banu Hashim and those who supported the Prophet (PBUH), lasting for three years.

Who served as the messenger for the Prophet (PBUH) and Hazrat Khadija (RA) before their marriage?

💡 Explanation

Nafisah bint Munabbih acted as the matchmaker and carried the proposal between Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) and Hazrat Khadija (RA).

Which country did the early Muslims migrate to when the persecution in Makkah became unbearable?

💡 Explanation

The first migration of Muslims took place to Abyssinia (Habsha), where the Negus (Najashi) provided them with protection.

Who was the maternal grandfather of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) who took his custody after the death of his mother, Aminah?

💡 Explanation

Abdul Muttalib was the grandfather of the Prophet (PBUH) who cared for him after the passing of his mother, Aminah bint Wahb.

Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) participated in a pre-Islamic alliance focused on establishing justice and protecting the weak, known as:

💡 Explanation

Hilf al-Fudul was an agreement made in Makkah by several clans to support the oppressed and establish justice, in which the young Muhammad (PBUH) participated.

Which uncle of the Prophet (PBUH) was his staunchest opponent and is specifically mentioned in Surah Al-Lahab?

💡 Explanation

Abu Lahab was the uncle of the Prophet (PBUH) who vehemently opposed him and was condemned in the Quran, specifically Surah Al-Lahab.

At what age did Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) receive his first revelation in the Cave of Hira?

💡 Explanation

The Prophet (PBUH) was 40 years old when he received the first revelation through Angel Jibrael (AS) in 610 AD.

Which companion is known as the 'Mu'adhdhin' of the Prophet (PBUH) and was freed from slavery by Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA) during the Makkan period?

💡 Explanation

Hazrat Bilal ibn Rabah (RA) was an Abyssinian slave who embraced Islam in Makkah and was severely persecuted by his master Umayyah ibn Khalaf before being bought and freed by Hazrat Abu Bakr (RA).

The Prophet (PBUH) preached Islam openly to the Makkans for the first time from which hill?

💡 Explanation

After receiving the command to warn his closest kinsmen, the Prophet (PBUH) ascended Mount Safa and publicly invited the Quraysh to Islam.

Who was the female companion who protected the Prophet (PBUH) during the Battle of Uhud, but is also remembered for her early support during the Makkan era, often called the mother of the Prophet?

💡 Explanation

Umm Ayman (Barakah) was the Prophet's (PBUH) childhood caregiver and remained a dedicated supporter throughout his life.

What was the name of the agreement signed between the Prophet (PBUH) and the people of Madinah (Ansar) before the migration, known as the 'Pledge of Allegiance'?

💡 Explanation

The Pledges of Aqabah were secret agreements between the Prophet (PBUH) and the delegations from Yathrib (Madinah) before the Hijrah.

During the Makkan period, who among the following was known for being the 'First Martyr' in Islam?

💡 Explanation

Sumayyah bint Khayyat is historically recognized as the first martyr in Islam, having been killed by Abu Jahl due to her faith.

The Prophet (PBUH) visited a city near Makkah in the 10th year of Prophethood to seek support, where he was stoned by the locals. Name the city:

💡 Explanation

The Prophet (PBUH) visited Taif to invite its leaders to Islam, but they rejected him and incited the youth to stone him, causing him significant injury.
