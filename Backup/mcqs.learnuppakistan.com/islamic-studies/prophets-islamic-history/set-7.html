The Abbasid Caliphate overthrew which dynasty in 750 AD?

💡 Explanation

The Abbasid Revolution resulted in the downfall of the Umayyad Caliphate in 750 AD.

Who was the founder of the Abbasid Caliphate?

💡 Explanation

Abu al-Abbas al-Saffah was the first caliph of the Abbasid dynasty, reigning from 750 to 754 AD.

Which city was built by the Abbasids to serve as their capital?

💡 Explanation

Baghdad was founded by the Caliph Al-Mansur in 762 AD and became the center of the Islamic world.

The period of the Abbasid Caliphate known as the 'Islamic Golden Age' reached its peak under which Caliph?

💡 Explanation

Harun al-Rashid's reign is traditionally associated with the peak of the Abbasid Golden Age, marked by scientific and cultural prosperity.

What was the name of the famous library and intellectual center established in Baghdad by the Abbasids?

💡 Explanation

Bayt al-Hikmah (House of Wisdom) was a major intellectual center in Baghdad during the Islamic Golden Age.

Which famous mathematician and astronomer is credited with the invention of Algebra during the Abbasid era?

💡 Explanation

Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi is known as the father of algebra and worked at the House of Wisdom.

The Abbasid Caliphate finally collapsed in 1258 AD due to the invasion of:

💡 Explanation

The Siege of Baghdad in 1258 by the Mongol forces under Hulagu Khan marked the end of the Abbasid Caliphate.

Which Caliph established the 'Bayt al-Hikmah' on a grand scale?

💡 Explanation

Caliph Al-Ma'mun is widely credited with significantly expanding the House of Wisdom and promoting translation of Greek works.

Which famous physician and philosopher, known as 'Avicenna' in the West, lived during the later Abbasid period?

💡 Explanation

Ibn Sina (Avicenna) was a polymath of the Abbasid period who wrote the Canon of Medicine.

Which battle in 751 AD between the Abbasids and the Tang Dynasty is significant for the introduction of paper making to the Islamic world?

💡 Explanation

The Battle of Talas facilitated the spread of papermaking technology from Chinese captives to the Abbasid Caliphate.

Which Abbasid Caliph is famously associated with the 'Mihna' (Inquisition) regarding the creation of the Quran?

💡 Explanation

Caliph Al-Ma'mun instituted the Mihna in 833 AD, requiring scholars to profess the Mu'tazilite doctrine that the Quran was created.

The Abbasid administrative division known as the 'Diwan' was primarily used for what purpose?

💡 Explanation

The Diwan was the central bureaucratic department established to manage state finances, land taxes, and civil service records.

Who was the legendary vizier family that played a pivotal role in the administration of the early Abbasid Caliphate under Harun al-Rashid?

💡 Explanation

The Barmakid family served as powerful viziers and advisors during the reigns of Al-Mahdi, Al-Hadi, and Harun al-Rashid.

Which city, located in modern-day Iraq, was constructed by Caliph Al-Mu'tasim to house his professional Turkish mercenary army?

💡 Explanation

Samarra served as the capital of the Abbasid Caliphate from 836 AD to 892 AD, built specifically to station the Turkish guard.

The Abbasid Caliphate saw the rise of the 'Mu'tazila' school of thought, which emphasized which of the following?

💡 Explanation

The Mu'tazilites were proponents of rationalism, arguing that human reason and free will were central to understanding faith.

Which literary masterpiece detailing animal fables, 'Kalila wa-Dimna', was translated from Pahlavi to Arabic during the Abbasid era by:

💡 Explanation

Ibn al-Muqaffa was a Persian scholar whose translation of Kalila wa-Dimna became a landmark of classical Arabic literature.

Which dynasty eventually rose in Egypt and challenged the Abbasid authority in the 10th century?

💡 Explanation

The Fatimid Caliphate, an Ismaili Shia dynasty, established itself in Egypt and rivaled the Abbasid Caliphs in Baghdad.

The 'Thousand and One Nights' (Alf Layla wa-Layla) reflects the cultural life of which Abbasid ruler?

💡 Explanation

Many of the stories in the Arabian Nights are set in the court of Harun al-Rashid, depicting the grandeur of the Abbasid golden age.

Which scholar is known as the 'Philosopher of the Arabs' and lived during the Abbasid Golden Age?

💡 Explanation

Al-Kindi is famously titled the 'Philosopher of the Arabs' for his work in synthesizing Greek philosophy with Islamic thought.

The Abbasid period witnessed the proliferation of paper mills after learning the technique from Chinese prisoners of war. Where was the first major paper mill in the Islamic world established?

💡 Explanation

Following the Battle of Talas in 751, the technology of papermaking was established first in Samarkand before spreading to Baghdad.
