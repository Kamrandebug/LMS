Which of the following is considered the primary foundation of Islamic social ethics according to the Quranic principle of 'Amr bil-Ma'ruf wa Nahy anil-Munkar'?

💡 Explanation

Amr bil-Ma'ruf wa Nahy anil-Munkar translates to enjoining what is right and forbidding what is wrong, which is the cornerstone of communal responsibility in Islam.

In Islamic social conduct, what does the concept of 'Haqooq-ul-Ibad' specifically emphasize?

💡 Explanation

Haqooq-ul-Ibad refers to the rights of the servants (human beings), emphasizing the importance of social justice, kindness, and fulfillment of duties towards others.

Which ethical principle in Islam discourages backbiting (Gheebat) and suspicion?

💡 Explanation

Hifz-ul-Lisan relates to the discipline of the tongue, which includes avoiding backbiting, slander, and unnecessary speech, as highlighted in Surah Al-Hujurat.

What is the meaning of 'Ihsan' in the context of Islamic conduct?

💡 Explanation

Ihsan is defined in the Hadith of Gabriel as acting in a way that suggests one is witnessing Allah, or at least being aware that Allah is witnessing one's actions, leading to moral excellence.

Which term describes the Islamic social virtue of 'moderation' or 'balance' in all affairs?

💡 Explanation

Wasatiyyah denotes the middle path or the balanced approach in behavior, spending, and social interaction, avoiding the extremes of excess or negligence.

According to Islamic ethics, what is the 'Amanah' (Trust) primarily associated with?

💡 Explanation

Amanah is a broad concept in Islam encompassing all responsibilities entrusted to a person, including moral, social, and spiritual obligations.

Which quality of social conduct does the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) specifically link to the completion of faith (Iman)?

💡 Explanation

The Prophet (PBUH) stated that the most perfect believers are those with the best character, emphasizing that Islam is inseparable from high moral conduct.

What does the term 'Adl' demand in social and judicial interactions?

💡 Explanation

Adl (Justice) in Islam requires absolute fairness and impartiality, as commanded in the Quran to stand for justice even if it is against oneself or kin.

In the context of social interaction, what does 'Hayah' signify?

💡 Explanation

Hayah is a central ethical concept representing modesty, humility, and a sense of shame, which prevents a believer from engaging in indecent speech or behavior.

What is the primary Islamic ethical requirement regarding the fulfillment of promises (Wafa bil-'Ahd)?

💡 Explanation

Fulfilling promises is considered a fundamental obligation in Islam, and breaching a covenant is categorized among the traits of hypocrisy (Nifaq).

Which Islamic ethical concept refers to the practice of 'preferring others over oneself' even when in need?

💡 Explanation

Ithar is the highest form of generosity where a person gives priority to others' needs above their own.

According to Islamic ethics, which act is considered the most virtuous form of Jihad, as per the Hadith of the Prophet (PBUH)?

💡 Explanation

The Prophet (PBUH) stated that the best Jihad is a word of justice before an oppressive ruler, emphasizing moral courage.

Which term in Islamic social conduct refers to the principle of 'forgiving those who have wronged you'?

💡 Explanation

Afw refers to the act of pardon and forgiveness, which is highly encouraged in the Quran as a sign of piety.

What does the concept of 'Silah-Rahmi' specifically dictate in Islamic social life?

💡 Explanation

Silah-Rahmi refers to the mandatory Islamic obligation of maintaining strong and kind relationships with blood relatives.

In Islamic business and social ethics, what does 'Tasahul' signify?

💡 Explanation

Tasahul refers to showing leniency, kindness, and ease when buying, selling, or settling debts with others.

Which attribute is considered the foundation of a healthy Islamic community, often described as 'smiling in your brother's face'?

💡 Explanation

Bashashat refers to a cheerful and welcoming countenance, which is considered a form of charity in Islamic social interaction.

What does the term 'Tawadu' entail in the context of Islamic social behavior?

💡 Explanation

Tawadu is the Islamic virtue of humility, being modest in behavior and avoiding arrogance or pride.

Which ethical principle requires a Muslim to refrain from asking about private matters of others that do not concern them?

💡 Explanation

Tajassus means prying or spying into the private affairs of others, which is strictly prohibited in Islamic conduct.

What is meant by the principle of 'Husn-e-Zann' in social interactions?

💡 Explanation

Husn-e-Zann refers to maintaining a good opinion of others and avoiding baseless suspicions.

Which of the following is the Islamic ethical term for 'contentment' with what Allah has provided?

💡 Explanation

Qana'ah is the internal peace and contentment derived from accepting one's decree, preventing social greed and envy.
